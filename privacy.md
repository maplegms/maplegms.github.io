# Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, self-hosted tool run by its owner on their own computer.

- It stores OAuth access tokens for the owner's own social media accounts, only on the owner's machine, solely to publish the owner's content.
- It does not collect, sell or share any personal data of other people.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising services are used.
- Tokens can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's settings, and all stored data is deleted by removing the app's local database.
